# Life at TOA Page Refresh

## Goal
Retain the authentic content from the original Life at TOA page while presenting it in a more polished, cinematic format consistent with the redesigned Team One Architects website.

## Page structure
- Keep the existing full-screen animated Life at TOA opening image and headline.
- Turn the introduction into a strong editorial statement with clearer visual hierarchy.
- Present the six culture stories as a varied scrolling narrative rather than repetitive text sections:
  - A Culture of Builders
  - Where Learning Is Real, Not Theoretical
  - Collaboration Without Boundaries
  - Driven by Scale, Built on Precision
  - Moments That Define Us
  - A Place to Grow, Not Just Work
- Use the existing Family Day photograph as the main culture image without repeating it unnecessarily.
- Give short principles and bullet points stronger typography, numbering, and structured spacing for easier reading.
- Reframe “The TOA Mindset” as a bold four-part statement band.
- Finish with the existing “Why TOA” message as the page’s closing statement.

## Visual and motion direction
- Preserve the site’s dark cinematic theme, TOA orange-gold accent, architectural grid language, and light-theme support.
- Use asymmetric editorial layouts, generous spacing, fine structural lines, and restrained image treatment.
- Add subtle scroll reveals and directional movement that support the story without distracting from it.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and ensure the layouts remain clear on mobile screens.

## Content integrity
- Use the wording and section order from the original Team One Architects page.
- Correct punctuation and line breaks only where needed for readability; do not invent workplace claims, activities, or statistics.

## Verification

- Confirm page metadata remains accurate and the project builds cleanly.
