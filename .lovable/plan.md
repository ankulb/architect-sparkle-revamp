1. The Economic Times
2. The Times of India
3. Hindustan Times
4. Realty+
5. ET Edge Insight

## Remaining coverage

- Present the remaining selected coverage in one continuous editorial grid, without month groupings or dates.
- Use publication logos and varied but disciplined card sizing to create a credible architecture-journal feel rather than a coverage counter or archive table.
- Keep publication and headline hierarchy consistent, with every card linking to its original article in a new tab.
- Normalize obvious publication-name spelling inconsistencies while preserving the published article headlines.

## Links and publication marks

- Research and verify the original article URL for every item retained on the page; do not add guessed or search-result links.
- Source each publication’s authentic logo from an official or reliable brand source, prepare it for legibility in both site themes, and store it through the project’s asset system.
- If an exact article cannot be verified, omit that item rather than leave a non-clickable or misleading card.
